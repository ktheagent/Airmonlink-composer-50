# Professional Workflow Gaps

## Immediate staff workflow

Build 40 does not provide a professional sequence of:

`place caret → choose duration → enter pitch → advance safely → add chord/voice/marking → undo`

Missing or defective elements:

- persistent note-entry mode;
- clear insertion caret with part, staff, voice, measure and beat;
- direct pitch entry from keyboard and staff position;
- automatic movement through measures;
- safe splitting and tying across barlines;
- same-onset chord input from active pitch controls;
- rhythmic dot and tuplet reflow;
- clear range selection;
- context-sensitive keypad state;
- friendly disabled-state guidance instead of engine exceptions.

## Professional page and engraving

The page service can produce physical pages, but professional output still lacks complete proof for:

- duration-aware horizontal spacing under real editing;
- automatic system casting after edits;
- collision avoidance across notes, beams, lyrics, dynamics and text;
- passage reflow without excessive empty space;
- editor/preview/PDF identity under complex scores;
- first-page publication hierarchy under real user content;
- linked-part page synchronization.

## Navigation

Build 40 exposes too many overlapping navigation layers:

- workspace modes;
- ribbon tabs;
- Composition Hub;
- command palette;
- inspector;
- piano dock;
- Staff and Sol-fa views.

Build 41 reduces the production surface. Later builds may restore a surface only when it shortens a verified workflow.
